# Memotown

A friend group's memories (notes and photos) build a small 3D town. Shared memories build modern apartments and skyscrapers downtown: every month is a building, every memory adds a floor, and anyone can name a building ("The best month of my life"). Solo memories grow that friend's lodge in the woods around the city. The town gets roads, parks and cars as the group shares more.

Items marked _(inferred)_ came from Claude, not the team. Confirm or correct them at the gate.

## Who it's for

A small friend group of three to six people who hang out in real life and already share moments in a group chat. Our own team of four is the first group.

**Who opens the town:** one friend from the group, on a phone or laptop, right after something happened. They just got home from a hangout, or they found a trip photo in their camera roll. Thirty seconds earlier they were in the group chat or the camera roll. _(inferred)_

## The job

Put a moment somewhere the whole group can see it, and see what the friendship has added up to so far.

## What it kills _(inferred)_

- Scrolling hundreds of group-chat messages to find "that photo from March".
- Shared albums nobody opens after the first week.
- "Remember when…" moments that nobody wrote down.

## Done

"I added last night's photo, our town grew a floor, and I tapped March to see everything we did that month."

## What it is not

- **Not a social network.** No public profiles, followers, likes or feed. Only members can see a town.
- **Not photo backup.** One photo per memory, up to 5 MB.
- **Not chat.** No comments, reactions or messages.
- **Not friend requests.** Friends join with an 8-character invite code.
- **Not people tagging.** Who was there is obvious: a shared memory means the group, a solo memory means just you.
- **Not several towns per person.** One town per person for now.
- **Not location, voice or video memories.** They are on the roadmap, not in this build.
- **Not live.** A friend's new memory shows up the next time you open or refresh the town.
- **Not editable.** Memories can't be edited or deleted in this build (known gap).

## Demo moment (30 seconds on stage)

1. Judges see a white town of six buildings, one per month from May to October 2026, with streets and a park. It is seeded with **19** of our group's real memories.
2. The presenter drags to pan and uses the zoom slider to swoop in.
3. They click the tallest building (for example "JUL 2026"). A side panel opens with that month's photos and notes.
4. They add a photo from today. The October building grows a floor in the presenter's color. That is the 20th memory, so **cars roll onto the streets** live on stage.

## How we'd know it worked

- **Today:** a friend who has never seen Memotown adds a memory without help in under 30 seconds. Judges can retell the idea in one sentence: "the town that grows from your memories."
- **After the hackathon:** groups add at least one memory a week, and people open the town just to look around, not only to post.
